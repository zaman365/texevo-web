# Public launch checklist

This is a review preview. Pending items are not implicit approval to spend, publish claims or contact customers.

- [ ] Confirm trading name/domain clearance, legal entity, address, representative and applicable register/tax details.
- [ ] Provide actual contact channels and response owner; approve public legal/privacy notices for the implemented processing.
- [ ] Qualify supplier routes and replace illustrative products with six or more verified, source-backed records.
- [ ] Review German copy, English production/contact routes, founder statements and all image rights; publish approved CMS content.
- [ ] Supply original authorised photography where required by the brief; retain accurate captions and evidence scope.
- [ ] Choose owned hosting/database/private-object accounts, processing regions, DPAs and any international specialist access arrangements.
- [ ] Configure HTTPS, upstream body/connection limits, trusted proxy handling, staff identity/MFA and least-privilege accounts.
- [ ] Connect CRM and transactional email gateways; verify durable acceptance, idempotency, assignment, acknowledgement and failure alerting.
- [ ] Configure the isolated scanner and private storage; test clean, infected, failed and oversized uploads, including unauthorized reads.
- [ ] Approve retention and deletion/export handling across application, CRM, sender, files and backups.
- [ ] Verify newsletter double opt-in, consent-version ordering, suppression and unsubscribe before enabling registration.
- [ ] Exercise restore/rollback on the actual purchased infrastructure and record RPO/RTO evidence.
- [ ] Conduct keyboard, screen reader, 200% zoom, real-phone, Safari/Firefox/Edge and buyer task testing.
- [ ] Measure production performance under throttling; validate real field vitals after sufficient traffic.
- [ ] Run `pnpm check:launch`, review all outstanding issues, then approve and set `SITE_MODE=live`.

The customer portal, live stock, online checkout, budget/delivery calculators and additional product categories remain separately gated phases. The present commit/push does not activate them or publish the preview to a production domain.
